Our Story

The art of blending cultures

FUEGO was born in the heart of Brickell with a clear vision: to celebrate the richness of Latin American cuisine through contemporary techniques and the finest quality ingredients.

Every dish is a sensory journey from the Peruvian Andes to the coasts of Colombia, passing through the vibrant culinary scene of Mexico City — reinterpreted with the elegance that Miami deserves.

Our executive chef, Alejandro Vega, brings over 15 years of experience at three-star restaurants in Lima, New York, and Barcelona.
